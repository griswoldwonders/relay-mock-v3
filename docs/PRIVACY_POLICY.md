# Relay Rider Privacy Policy

**Planning draft — transportation and privacy counsel review required**  
**Effective date:** August 5, 2026

Relay Rider is an institution-sponsored commuter coordination and Transportation Demand Management platform developed by Common Pathways Technologies. This policy describes information used in the product prototype and a future controlled beta. Relay Rider is not an on-demand ride-hailing, taxi, shuttle, instant-pickup, or guaranteed transportation service.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to Relay Rider websites, prototypes, controlled-beta applications, surveys, institution-sponsored commuter programs, program administration, support, and safety reporting.

## 2. Information We May Collect

Relay Rider may collect:

- Name, email, phone number, and account credentials.
- Institution, worksite, campus, participant role, department, and cohort affiliation.
- Approximate origin and destination zones.
- Recurring commute days, morning and return windows, and schedule flexibility.
- Current commute mode and parking-difficulty signals.
- Planned-route patterns, capacity, detour preferences, and route availability.
- Vehicle information and EV/hybrid status.
- Access Point preferences and walking-distance tolerance.
- Accessibility requests and service-animal accommodation needs.
- Privacy, communication, and research preferences.
- Program eligibility, participation, and institution-sponsored benefit records.
- Verification status and limited verification results.
- Communications, support requests, incident reports, and participant feedback.
- Device, browser, diagnostic, and product-usage information.

Precise geolocation should be collected only when needed for an approved workflow and with appropriate notice and consent.

## 3. How We Use Information

Relay Rider may use information to:

- Determine program and cohort eligibility.
- Generate commuter-option previews.
- Evaluate route overlap, schedule compatibility, estimated detour, and Access Point fit.
- Support participant consent and administrative review.
- Administer institution-sponsored incentives and program caps.
- Support participant communications.
- Maintain safety, support, blocking, reporting, suspension, and incident workflows.
- Produce aggregated or de-identified TDM reports.
- Improve the product prototype or controlled beta.
- Meet legal, accounting, insurance, and institutional obligations.

## 4. Location Privacy and Progressive Disclosure

Relay Rider uses approximate zones before precise locations.

1. During intake, general zones and time windows should be used.
2. During match preview, participants should see generalized routes and proposed Access Points.
3. After mutual consent and administrative approval, only information needed for coordination should be disclosed.
4. Precise details should be deleted or de-identified when they are no longer required under the applicable retention schedule.

Exact home addresses should not be displayed during intake or match preview.

A sponsoring institution may receive program eligibility, participation, review, incentive, and aggregated reporting information. The product should clearly identify which information is visible to the institution and which information remains private.

## 6. Information Sharing

Information may be shared with:

- Sponsoring institutions and authorized program administrators.
- Identity, eligibility, vehicle, or verification providers.
- Hosting, analytics, communications, and customer-support providers.
- Safety, accessibility, legal, insurance, and accounting advisors.
- Government authorities or other parties when legally required or necessary to protect rights and safety.

Relay Rider does not intend to sell participant personal information or use commute-location information for cross-context behavioral advertising. This statement must remain consistent with the actual analytics, advertising, and vendor configuration.

## 7. Retention

Relay Rider intends to retain information only as long as needed for the relevant program, participant request, safety matter, accounting obligation, dispute, legal requirement, or institutional agreement.

Proposed retention periods remain **[NEEDS COUNSEL INPUT]** and should be documented in a separate retention schedule before operational activation.

## 8. Participant Choices and Rights

Participants may request, where applicable:

- Access to their information.
- Correction of inaccurate information.
- Deletion.
- Portability.
- Withdrawal from a program.
- Withdrawal of optional research or marketing consent.
- Communication-preference changes.
- Changes to location precision.
- Temporary profile hiding.
- Blocking or reporting of another participant.

Certain records may need to be retained for legal, accounting, insurance, safety, or program-administration reasons.

## 9. Security

Relay Rider intends to use reasonable safeguards, including encryption in transit, role-based access, least-privilege permissions, logging, vendor review, retention controls, and incident-response procedures. No system can guarantee absolute security.

## 10. Adults Only

The current prototype and controlled-beta concept are intended for adult participants. A separate attorney-reviewed policy and operating model would be required before any program involving unaccompanied minors.

## 11. Updates

Relay Rider may update this policy as the product, legal requirements, institutional programs, and data practices change. Material updates should be communicated to affected participants and consent should be refreshed when required.
