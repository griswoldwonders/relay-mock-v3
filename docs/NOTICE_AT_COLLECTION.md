# Relay Rider Notice at Collection

**Planning draft — counsel review required**  
**Effective date:** August 5, 2026

Relay Rider collects information to operate an institution-sponsored commuter coordination and Transportation Demand Management prototype or controlled beta.

## Categories of Information

Depending on the form or workflow, Relay Rider may collect:

- Contact and account information.
- Institution, worksite, campus, role, department, and cohort affiliation.
- Approximate origin and destination zones.
- Recurring commute days, time windows, and flexibility.
- Current commute mode and parking-difficulty signals.
- Planned-route, capacity, detour, and availability information.
- Vehicle and EV/hybrid information.
- Access Point, walking-distance, accessibility, privacy, and communication preferences.
- Program eligibility, participation, and institution-sponsored benefit records.
- Support, feedback, and incident information.
- Device and product-usage information.

## Purposes

Relay Rider uses this information to:

- Determine program eligibility.
- Generate and explain commuter-option previews.
- Evaluate schedule, route, detour, capacity, Access Point, accessibility, and cohort compatibility.
- Support participant consent and administrative review.
- Administer institution-sponsored benefits and program limits.
- Support safety, privacy, accessibility, and incident workflows.
- Produce aggregated or de-identified TDM reporting.
- Improve the product prototype or controlled beta.

## Location Privacy

Relay Rider uses approximate zones before precise locations. Exact home addresses should not be displayed during intake or match preview. More precise information should be collected or disclosed only when needed for an approved workflow and with appropriate consent.

## Retention and Sharing

Information may be shared with authorized program administrators and service providers as described in the Privacy Policy. Retention periods remain subject to legal, insurance, accounting, and institutional review.

## Your Choices

Optional research and marketing consent should be separate from required program and privacy acknowledgments. Participants may request access, correction, deletion, consent withdrawal, communication changes, or withdrawal from the program, subject to applicable retention requirements.
