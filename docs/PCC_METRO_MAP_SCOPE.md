# PCC + Metro Map Scope

This map scope is intentionally prototype-safe and institution-focused.

## Metro A Line focus

Relay Rider highlights the Pasadena A Line station spine most relevant to PCC commute coordination: Del Mar, Memorial Park, Lake, Allen, and Sierra Madre Villa. The connecting line is schematic station-to-station context, not exact track geometry or real-time rail routing.

## PCC shuttle focus

Published PCC shuttle boarding context is represented for:

- Colorado Campus: northeast side between parking lots 6 and 7 (approximate map pin)
- Allen Avenue A Line Station: curbside in front of the station at the PCC Shuttle Stop sign
- Foothill Campus: Parking Lot C (approximate campus pin)

PCC also identifies Jefferson Training Center as part of its shuttle-served campus network, but the current transportation page does not publish the precise Jefferson boarding point. The prototype therefore shows the Jefferson site with `boarding location pending confirmation` and does not invent a precise drop-off location.

## Product guardrails

- Metro and shuttle lines are map context, not guaranteed itineraries.
- PCC shuttle eligibility, schedules, and operations remain subject to PCC rules and current published service.
- Candidate Access Points remain subject to administrative/site review.
- Pins do not imply institutional approval for Relay Rider pickup/dropoff activity.
- Approximate map pins are used where official source material describes a boarding area but does not publish coordinates.
