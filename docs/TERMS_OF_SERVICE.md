# Relay Rider Terms of Service

**Planning draft — transportation, privacy, and contract counsel review required**  
**Effective date:** August 5, 2026

## 1. Product Description

Relay Rider is an institution-sponsored commuter coordination and Transportation Demand Management platform developed by Common Pathways Technologies. It supports commuter intake, planned-route registration, compatibility previews, Access Point information, administrative review, institution-sponsored benefits, and modeled reporting.

Relay Rider is not an on-demand ride-hailing, taxi, shuttle, instant-pickup, live dispatch, or guaranteed transportation service.

## 2. Eligibility

Current controlled-beta participation is limited to eligible adults in an approved institution, participant group, or cohort. Participants must provide accurate information, comply with program-specific rules, and maintain only one account unless a program administrator authorizes otherwise.

## 3. Controlled-Beta Status

The current product is a prototype or controlled-beta environment. Features may be simulated, modeled, incomplete, changed, suspended, or removed. Participation is limited to approved corridors and participant groups.

## 4. No Commuter Charge During Beta

Approved commuter participants are not charged by Relay Rider to participate in the current controlled beta. This beta policy does not guarantee that a commuter option will be available or operate.

Relay Rider will provide notice and obtain any required agreement before introducing a future fee, proposed contribution, payment, or purchase workflow.

## 5. Commuter-Option Limitations

A commuter option is a compatibility preview, not a reservation or transportation purchase.

- Compatibility and route-fit scores are estimates.
- Detour, walking, and arrival estimates are not guaranteed.
- Capacity may change.
- Access Points remain subject to program, site, accessibility, and administrative review.
- Participation requires mutual consent.
- Administrative review may be required.
- Either participant may decline before confirmation.
- Relay Rider does not guarantee route acceptance or operation.

## 6. Planned Routes

A participant registering a planned route confirms that the trip is already intended to occur. Relay Rider does not dispatch participants to create trips on demand.

A planned-route participant may define availability, capacity, schedule, Access Point, privacy, verification, and detour preferences and may accept or decline compatible interest.

## 7. Institution-Sponsored Benefits

A sponsoring institution may offer capped promotional benefits under separate program rules. Eligibility may depend on verification, participation, program limits, budget availability, and administrative approval.

Benefits are not wages, fares, guaranteed earnings, certified carbon credits, LCFS credits, utility credits, or guaranteed reimbursements. Relay Rider or the institution may correct, suspend, expire, deny, or reverse a benefit recorded in error, subject to applicable law and program terms.

## 8. Access Points

Access Points may be proposed, reviewed, designated, or institutionally approved. Relay Rider does not guarantee the safety, availability, lighting, accessibility, or suitability of any location.

Participants must follow site rules and may not enter restricted property without authorization.

## 9. Participant Conduct

Participants may not:

- Harass, threaten, discriminate against, or harm another participant.
- Participate while impaired, dangerously fatigued, unlicensed, uninsured, or otherwise ineligible.
- Provide false identity, institution, route, vehicle, capacity, or schedule information.
- Arrange unauthorized payments or off-program commercial activity.
- Reveal another participant's personal or location information without authorization.
- Circumvent program controls, verification, administrative review, blocking, or suspension.
- Use Relay Rider for unlawful activity.

## 10. Accessibility and Nondiscrimination

Relay Rider intends to provide an accessibility-request pathway and maintain a nondiscrimination policy. The prototype does not promise that every vehicle, Access Point, or route is accessible.

Unresolved accommodation requests should be reviewed before coordination. Service-animal and accessibility procedures require counsel and institution review before operational activation.

## 11. Privacy

Use of Relay Rider is subject to the Relay Rider Privacy Policy and applicable notices at collection. Participants should review how approximate zones, schedules, institution affiliation, and program records are used and disclosed.

## 12. Incidents and Emergencies

Call emergency services first in an emergency. Relay Rider is not an emergency-response service.

Participants should report safety, privacy, accessibility, conduct, property, or route incidents through the applicable program channel. Relevant records may be preserved or shared with authorized administrators, insurers, counsel, service providers, or authorities as permitted by law.

## 13. Suspension and Termination

Relay Rider or a sponsoring institution may suspend a participant, pause a route, remove a match preview, revoke program eligibility, preserve relevant records, or restrict features when needed to enforce program rules, investigate an incident, protect participants, manage program capacity, or comply with law.

A participant contact or appeal pathway should be established before operational activation.

## 14. Changes to the Service or Terms

Relay Rider may modify the prototype, controlled beta, program rules, or these terms. Material changes should be communicated to affected participants and renewed acceptance should be obtained when required.
