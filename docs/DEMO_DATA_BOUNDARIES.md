# Demo Data Boundaries

Institutional demonstration views may contain believable local/demo data for Pasadena, Eagle Rock, and Glendale. All such records and modeled outcomes must be labeled as DEMO DATA, MODELED, ESTIMATED, SIMULATED, or Sample Program.

Participant research-beta intake is separate and must not be seeded with fabricated commuter records.

Institution names on the map or in demonstration dashboards are geographic/institutional context only and do not imply Relay Rider customers, partnerships, pilots, regulatory approval, or endorsements.
