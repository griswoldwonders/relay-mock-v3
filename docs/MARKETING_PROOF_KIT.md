## LinkedIn post — ready to publish

**A commuter dashboard should not start with charts. It should start with geography.**

Employers, campuses and other institutions need to understand where commute demand is coming from, when people are traveling, where parking pressure appears, and what existing planned routes or transit connections may overlap.

We have added a real interactive map to the Relay Rider product prototype for the Pasadena ↔ Eagle Rock ↔ Glendale corridor. The map is designed to put research anchors, candidate Access Points and multimodal context around the rest of the workflow:

**Signal → Record → Score → Preview → Task → Review → Dashboard → Report → Partner Action**

The goal is not to promise a ride. It is to help an institution understand what the evidence suggests it should review next — for example, collect more data, recruit planned-route participants, evaluate an Access Point candidate, test an incentive scenario, or continue monitoring.

Current environment: product prototype / demonstration environment. Candidate locations are not automatically approved or guaranteed suitable. Modeled outputs are labeled as modeled.

## Buyer-specific opening lines

### Employer / ETC

Relay Rider helps employers turn commute-pattern, parking-pressure and planned-route signals into traceable TDM tasks, decision cards and partner-ready reporting.

### Campus

Relay Rider helps campuses understand recurring commuter corridors, parking pressure, Access Point willingness and EV/hybrid participation before committing resources to a mobility intervention.

### Hospital

Relay Rider can organize shift-based commute demand, parking difficulty and charging-interest signals into corridor findings for workforce, facilities and sustainability teams.

### Municipality / business district

Relay Rider provides a controlled way to compare commuter demand, parking pressure, planned-route availability and modeled interventions across a defined corridor without operating a public transportation marketplace.

### EV / sustainability

Relay Rider organizes arrival windows, dwell periods and opt-in charging-interest signals so institutions can determine whether a formal workplace-charging or clean-mobility assessment is warranted.
